Why this approach?

EPA school guidance recommends assessing school conditions, identifying priorities, establishing baselines and tracking measurable indicators such as energy and water use. Greenovation adapts that evidence-first idea into a student-friendly scorecard.

Source: U.S. EPA school environmental guidance.

How improvement is proven

Greenovation separates programme activity from environmental performance. Actions, assignments and points are shown live as they happen. A percentage improvement is shown only when the same environmental indicator has a documented baseline and a later comparable measurement.

This evidence-first approach follows established school-environment guidance: establish a baseline, track measurable indicators and compare results over time. Source: U.S. EPA school environmental guidance.
